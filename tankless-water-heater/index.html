Tankless Water Heater Installation and Service in Burbank, IL

A tankless heater heats water only when you open a tap, so there is no tank to rust through and no standby loss. The tradeoffs are a larger upfront cost and a gas line and vent that must be sized correctly.

Is Tankless Right for Your House?

A tankless unit suits households that want continuous hot water and wall space back. It is less suited to a home where several showers, a dishwasher and a washing machine run at once and the unit is undersized.

The question is flow rate at the temperature rise your water needs. Winter groundwater is cold in Illinois, so the unit must raise the temperature more than it would in a warm climate.

Worth knowing

Hardness Makes Descaling Part of the Plan.

Chicago reported hardness of 134 to 143 mg/L as calcium carbonate in its 2013 quarterly lab reports, and that water reaches Burbank through the South Stickney Sanitary District. It is a 2013 figure, so confirm the current value.

Scale inside a tankless heat exchanger cuts flow and triggers error codes. Flushing with service valves keeps the unit efficient, and many homeowners schedule a flush yearly.

Tankless Service We Perform.

Existing tankless owners call for these jobs.

Flush and descale.

A pump circulates a descaling solution through the heat exchanger, and the unit is flushed with clean water. It restores flow and clears scale-related error codes.

Error code repair.

Ignition faults, flame sensor problems and flow sensor failures are diagnosed against the unit's code list.

Venting and condensate.

Blocked vents and clogged condensate lines shut condensing units down. We clear and test them.

Recirculation setup.

A recirculation pump shortens the wait for hot water at distant fixtures when installed with the right controls.

Venting and Gas Standards for Tankless Units.

Tankless gas units are installed against the manufacturer instructions and NFPA 54. Venting is the detail most often wrong, because condensing units use plastic vent pipe and non-condensing units need metal.

Gas line size matters too. A line that was sized for a tank heater may be too small for a unit with a higher burner input.
