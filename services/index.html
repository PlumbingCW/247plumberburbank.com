How to Pick the Right Service.

Start with the symptom, as plumbing guidelines recommend. A drain that is slow is a different call than a sewer line that has stopped moving, and a leaking water heater needs a different crew than a hidden leak in a wall.

If you are not sure, call the line. Dispatch listens to the problem and tells you which service applies and what the first visit will accomplish.
