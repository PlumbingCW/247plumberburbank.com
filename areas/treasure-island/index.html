What Treasure Island Means in Burbank.

Treasure Island appears as a Burbank subdivision on real estate sites such as Homes by Marco and Neighborhoods.com, and NeighborhoodScout labels a Burbank area "Treasure Island / City Center." Listings tied to the name sit on McVicker, Melvina and Austin Avenues. Others sit on West 80th Street, West 79th Place and West 80th Place.

The City of Burbank does not use the name on its history page or in its 2023 newsletters, and no source we found draws a boundary. We therefore treat Treasure Island as a local label, not an official district, and describe it by those anchor streets.

The Housing Around These Streets.

Listing data for Treasure Island describes single-family homes built between 1953 and 1971, with three to five bedrooms and sizes from about 1,000 to 3,650 square feet. That data comes from a real estate feed and the site itself calls it unverified, so we use it only as a rough picture.

It fits the wider city. The City of Burbank reports that the area had its first water and sewer system by 1959, and Burbank is about 90 percent residential according to the Cook County hazard mitigation plan.

Why We Do Not Guess at Your Pipes.

A house built between the 1950s and 1970s can have original pipes, replaced pipes or a mix of both. Nothing we found tells us which materials are under any Treasure Island street, so we do not assume.

A camera inspection or a look at the pipes in your own basement answers the question for your house in a few minutes.

Who Handles Which Part of a Sewer Backup.

The South Stickney Sanitary District delivers water and maintains sewers in its district, except lines owned by individual property owners or the Metropolitan Water Reclamation District. The district says owners are responsible for the line from the house to the main.

Storms, Basements and Sump Pumps.

The Cook County hazard mitigation plan lists residential streets in Burbank where flooding still happens in larger storms, and it records federal flood declarations in 2013 and 2023. One listed stretch, 83rd Street between Narragansett and Melvina Avenues, ends at a street that also appears in Treasure Island listings, but the plan does not name the neighborhood and we do not claim that its homes are affected.

What we can say is general. Burbank does not allow a sump pump to discharge into the sanitary sewer, so the discharge line should go outdoors. Our basement waterproofing page lists the options.
