What Condo Plumbing Changes.

A leak in a condo can reach the unit below, so the first step is the same as anywhere: shut the fixture valve or the unit shutoff, then call. After that, tell your neighbors and the association, because they may need to check a ceiling or a shared line.

A drain that backs up in several units points to a shared stack, which is a building problem. A drain that backs up in only one unit usually points to your own branch line.

Before the Plumber Arrives at Your Unit.

A little preparation avoids delays in a multi-unit building.

Tell management.

Ask whether the association needs notice, a certificate of insurance or a time window for contractors.

Find your shutoffs.

Locate the valve under the fixture and, if there is one, the unit shutoff.

Clear the space.

Move items from under sinks and around the water heater.

Plan for parking.

Ask where a service van can park near your building.
