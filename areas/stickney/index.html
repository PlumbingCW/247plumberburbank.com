Burbank's Neighbor in Stickney Township.

Stickney was incorporated on December 13, 1913, and had a 2020 Census population of 7,110. It sits in Stickney Township, the same township as Burbank, and Village Hall is at 6533 Pershing Road.

The village saw its main homebuilding boom after World War II, so many properties have drain and supply lines that are decades old.

Common Plumbing Problems in Postwar Homes.

Houses built in the decades after the war share certain plumbing habits.

Cast iron and clay drains.

Old drain lines rust or crack, and roots find the joints.

Galvanized supply pipe.

Steel supply lines corrode and narrow, which leads to low pressure and rusty water.

Small basement fixtures.

Utility sinks, floor drains and laundry hookups are where backups first show up.

Aging water heaters.

Tanks installed decades ago may leak at any time.

Lead Service Lines in the Village Inventory.

Stickney's published water system inventory lists lead service lines among the materials in the village system. The village water department can be reached at (708) 749-4400.

If your service line may be lead, tell dispatch before we plan work. We will route a line replacement through the right approvals. See water service line repair for what to expect.

Living Near a Major Wastewater Plant.

The Metropolitan Water Reclamation District operates the Stickney Water Reclamation Plant nearby, which is described as among the largest wastewater treatment plants. It treats the flows of a large share of Chicago and surrounding suburbs.

The plant does not make your sewer line any more or less likely to back up, but it is a reminder that the public system is large and shared. Private lines are the homeowner's responsibility, and our crews clear and repair them.

Before We Arrive at Your Stickney Home.

Permits and Codes in Stickney.

The Village of Stickney handles plumbing permits, and the work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard found in 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890 and administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health.

We check with the village before work that needs an inspection.
