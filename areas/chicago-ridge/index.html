A Small Village With Big Corridors.

Chicago Ridge was incorporated on October 17, 1914, and had a 2020 Census population of 14,433. Its neighbors include Oak Lawn, Bridgeview, Palos Hills and Worth.

Chicago Ridge Mall at 95th Street and Ridgeland Avenue draws traffic from across the southwest suburbs, and a 135-acre industrial park on Southwest Highway at 103rd Street adds commercial plumbing work to the mix.

Permits and Contractors in Chicago Ridge.

Chicago Ridge says any plumbing work needs a permit, and contractors must be registered, insured and bonded. Village Hall is at 10455 S Ridgeland Avenue, and the number is 708-425-7700. Hours are Monday through Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and Saturday from 9 a.m. to noon.

We are licensed and insured, and dispatch will explain how the permit step works for your repair. Ask before work starts if you want the village's requirements confirmed.

Water and Creeks Around the Village.

Chicago Ridge receives Lake Michigan water as a downstream purchaser, meaning the water reaches it through neighboring systems that buy from the City of Chicago. If your tap water changes after work on a main, run the cold tap for a few minutes.

Stony Creek runs through the area, and heavy rain can fill local drains quickly. Basement seepage after a storm is often a drainage issue that a sump pump or a backwater valve can handle. See basement waterproofing for the options.

Before We Arrive at Your Chicago Ridge Home.

These steps get the visit off to a quick start.

Permits and Codes in Chicago Ridge.

Plumbing in Chicago Ridge follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard found in 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890 and administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health, and the Village of Chicago Ridge handles permits.
