Our Closest Neighbor to the West.

Bridgeview was incorporated in 1947 and had a 2020 Census population of 17,027. The village borders Burbank, Oak Lawn, Chicago Ridge, Hickory Hills, Justice and Bedford Park, and Harlem Avenue forms its eastern edge.

Because we are a short drive away, Bridgeview calls are among the most direct routes on our map.

What Bridgeview Homeowners Call About.

Bridgeview has a mix of older homes and newer commercial development. These are the issues that fill the line.

  • Sewer backups into basement floor drains after heavy rain.
  • Water heater leaks and no-hot-water calls.
  • Burst pipes during winter cold snaps.
  • Slow drains that return after a snake.
  • Sump pumps that fail during storms.

Each problem has its own page. Backups are covered by sewer line cleaning, and failed tanks by water heater replacement.

Village Rules Worth Knowing.

Bridgeview has its own flood regulations and a sewer use ordinance, which set how wastewater and stormwater are handled on private property. The details differ from Burbank, so ask dispatch if you are unsure how a repair or discharge is allowed in the village.

The village buys Lake Michigan water directly from the City of Chicago. If you see discolored water after street work, run a cold tap for a few minutes and call the village water department if it persists.

Backup in Bridgeview: Village Main or Your Line?

The first question on a backup call is whose pipe is blocked.

Likely the village main.

  • Neighbors are backing up too.
  • Several fixtures overflow at once.
  • The street drain is also flooding.
  • The village public works can check it.

Likely your own line.

  • Only your house is affected.
  • The problem began with one fixture.
  • Roots or grease are the history.
  • A plumber can clear and camera it.

Not sure? Dispatch can run a cable and a camera to confirm. See sewer camera inspection.

Before We Arrive at Your Bridgeview Home.

Small actions make the first visit more effective.

Stop using water.

Every flush and every load of laundry adds to a backup.

Protect the floor.

Move belongings off the basement floor and photograph any damage.

Locate the shutoff.

Know where the main water valve is in case a pipe is leaking.

Have the address ready.

Give the full address and ZIP, and a cross street if your block is hard to find.

Permits and Codes in Bridgeview.

The Village of Bridgeview issues permits for plumbing work, and the work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard found in 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890 and administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health.

We check with the village before a job that needs an inspection so the schedule holds.
