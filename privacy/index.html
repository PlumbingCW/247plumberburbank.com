Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we receive when you use this website or contact us, and how we handle it.

Talk to dispatch
(888) 910-8644Call (888) 910-8644

What We Collect.

This website has no forms. When you call us we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us about the job, such as your name, address and the problem. When you email us we receive your email address and the message.

We use this information to schedule visits, answer questions and follow up on completed work.

Third-Party Services on the Site.

Pages on this site load fonts from Google Fonts and may display a Google Maps embed. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads them. Their own privacy policies explain how they handle that data.

We do not sell your information.

How We Use and Share Information.

We use your information to provide plumbing services and to communicate with you. We share it only with people who help us do the job, such as a technician or a supplier, and when the law requires it.

We keep job records for as long as we need them for warranty, tax and legal reasons.

Your Choices.

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@247plumberburbank.com with your request.

Phone numbers can be removed from our contact list at any time.
