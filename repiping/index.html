Whole-House Repiping in Burbank, IL

When water pressure is poor, rusty water shows up and leaks keep appearing in different places, repiping can solve the problem at its root. Burbank 24/7 Plumbing replaces failing supply piping with new copper or PEX.

The work is planned to disrupt the house as little as possible, and we explain every choice.

Signs That It Is Time to Repipe.

A single leak is a repair. A pattern of leaks, rust-colored water and falling pressure is a sign the pipes are wearing out together. Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside and narrows, which is why pressure drops long before pipes burst.

If you have repaired the same line more than once, a camera look and a pressure test show whether replacement makes more sense than another patch.

Symptoms We See in Aging Plumbing.

These are the usual calls that lead to a repipe estimate.

Low pressure at every fixture.

Corrosion has narrowed the pipe. Replacing a fixture will not fix it.

Brown or rusty water.

The inside of old steel is flaking. It is worst after the water sits overnight.

Repeated pinhole leaks.

The metal is thinning in many places. Each patch just moves the failure.

Visible green or white buildup.

Corrosion on joints and fittings signals aging pipe and failing connections.

Worth knowing

Lead Risk in Older Chicago-Area Homes.

Chicago, which supplies water to the district serving Burbank, states in its 2024 water quality report that homes built before 1986 likely have lead service lines. A repipe inside the house does not replace the service line outside, so ask the South Stickney Sanitary District about the line material.

If you plan to repipe, it is a good time to find out what the line from the street is made of and to plan a replacement of it as well. See water service line repair.

How a Repipe Is Done.

A repipe is a project with a plan.

1

Survey.

We trace the existing lines, find access points and plan new routes.

2

Rough-in.

New pipe is run through walls, floors and the basement with careful, small openings.

3

Connect.

Fixtures and the water heater are connected to the new system and tested under pressure.

4

Repair.

Access openings are patched so that a drywall finisher can complete them.

Permits and Inspection.

The City of Burbank Building Department requires a permit for alteration of plumbing, and all contractors must be licensed. Contact the department at 708-599-5500, option 2, at 6530 W 79th Street.

A repipe passes through inspection like any other plumbing project, and we schedule it with the city.

What to Do Before a Repipe Crew Arrives.

Preparation keeps people and belongings safe.

  • Clear walls, closets and cabinets that sit on the pipe route.
  • Fill containers with water for use while the supply is off.
  • Cover floors and furniture near the work.
  • Keep children and pets away from open walls.
  • Tell the crew about any known asbestos or lead paint in the walls.

If you see mold or crumbling insulation, tell the crew before they cut.

Materials and Permits for a Repipe.

PEX tubing is made to ASTM F876 and copper tubing to ASTM B88. Both are accepted under the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard found in 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890 and administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health, and a repipe needs a permit and an inspection in Burbank.

The City of Chicago has stated that homes built before 1986 may have lead service lines, so older piping is worth checking, not assuming the worst.
