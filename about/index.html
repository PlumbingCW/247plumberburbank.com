About Burbank 24/7 Plumbing

Burbank 24/7 Plumbing is an emergency plumbing company based at 5822 W 79th Street in Burbank, Illinois. We answer the line around the clock and serve Burbank and five nearby towns.

This page explains how we work and what you can expect.

What We Do.

We repair and install plumbing for homes and small businesses. The work ranges from clearing a clogged drain to replacing a water heater, repairing a sewer line and installing a sump pump.

Emergency calls come first because that is when people need a plumber most. Planned work is scheduled around the emergency load.

How We Work.

Every call starts with dispatch. They ask about the problem, the address and any water that is spreading. They schedule the next available visit and explain the price on the phone.

On site, the plumber diagnoses the problem, confirms the price and asks you to approve it before work begins.

Pricing Without Surprises.

We believe a price should be clear before work starts. That means we do not start repairs and quote later. If the scope changes after we open a wall, we stop and explain the new price.

The same rule holds at night and on weekends.

Licensed and Insured.

Our plumbers are licensed and the company is insured. Illinois licenses plumbers through the Illinois Department of Public Health, and the department provides a public tool to verify a license. Ask dispatch if you want to confirm credentials before a visit.

The City of Burbank also requires that plumbing contractors be licensed and that a permit be obtained for the construction or alteration of plumbing.

Why We Pay Attention to Local Details.

A plumber working in Burbank should know that the South Stickney Sanitary District maintains the sewer mains and delivers water, that the owner is responsible beyond the main and that sump pumps must not discharge into the sanitary sewer. Those facts shape how we diagnose and what we advise.

We study local sources such as the City of Burbank, the Metropolitan Water Reclamation District and the Cook County hazard mitigation plan, and we write what we find into our service pages. According to the Illinois Department of Public Health, plumbing work must follow the state plumbing code, and we hold ourselves to that standard.

What Happens When You Call.

A person answers the line, not a menu. Dispatch asks for your address, what you see or hear and whether water is spreading. If water is flowing, they tell you how to shut off the main valve while the crew is on the way.

Then they schedule the next available visit and tell you what to expect. Same-day appointments are offered when available, and we do not promise an exact arrival time because traffic and earlier calls change the schedule.

Honest About What a Visit Can Do.

The first visit finds the cause. Sometimes the plumber can fix it on the spot, and sometimes the problem needs a part or a second visit. We tell you which before you approve anything.

We would rather say that a repair is sound than sell a replacement that is not needed. If a fixture or a water heater is near the end of its life, we explain why and let you decide.

Homes and Businesses We Serve.

Most calls come from single-family homes, two-flats and small buildings. We also serve small shops, offices and restaurants, where a stopped drain or a failed water heater can close the doors for the day.

Our commercial plumbing page explains how we handle those calls, and each service page explains the work in plain terms.

How We Keep Our Information Current.

Plumbing rules and local facts change. Codes are updated, districts revise their policies and phone numbers move. When we cite a source on a service page, we tell you which one so you can check it yourself.

If you spot something on this site that looks out of date, call or email us. We would rather correct a page than leave a wrong detail in front of a neighbor.

Where We Work.

Our core area is Burbank, ZIP 60459. We also serve Oak Lawn, Bridgeview, Chicago Ridge, Hickory Hills and Stickney. Each has its own page listing the ZIP codes we cover. See the service areas page and the full list of services.

If you are outside those areas, call the line. If a crew cannot reach you in a reasonable time, we will say so.
