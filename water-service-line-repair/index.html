The service line is the pipe that carries water from the street to your meter. When it leaks or breaks you may see a wet yard, hear water running or lose pressure and water altogether.

No Water or a Wet Yard: Where to Start.

If water stops entirely, check whether neighbors have water and whether the main shutoff inside the house is open. If water runs but pressure has dropped sharply, a service line leak is a likely cause.

A wet patch in the yard that stays soggy in dry weather, or a sound of running water near the street side of the house, points to a leak in the service line. Call the 24/7 line and describe what you notice.

Lead Service Lines in Older Chicago-Area Homes.

Chicago, which sells water to the district, states in its 2024 water quality report that homes built before 1986 likely have lead service lines. The material of your service line should be confirmed with the district, because a repair on a lead line is an opportunity to replace it.

If your line is lead or galvanized, tell dispatch. Replacing the line rather than patching it may be the better long-term choice, and the district's notices describe the requirements.

Repair Methods for Service Lines.

The repair depends on depth, material and what lies above.

Spot repair.

A hole is dug at the leak and the damaged section is replaced with new pipe and fittings.

Full line replacement.

When the line is old or failing in several places, a full replacement from the street connection to the meter gives a clean start.

Trenchless options.

In some cases the new line is pulled through with limited digging, which protects driveways and landscaping.

Low Water Pressure Is Not Always the Line.

Low pressure can come from a partly closed main valve, a failing pressure regulator or a clogged fixture, not only a damaged service line. We test pressure at an outdoor spigot and at fixtures to isolate the cause before recommending a repair.

Hard-water scale inside old galvanized piping also strangles flow over the years. If only one fixture is weak, a cartridge or aerator cleaning may fix it.

Standards and Lead Service Lines.

The line from the main to the meter carries drinking water, so materials must meet NSF/ANSI 61. The City of Chicago has published a statement that homes built before 1986 may have a lead service line, and the same caution is wise for older homes in nearby towns.

A water service repair requires a permit, and the utility may need to be notified before the street side is touched.
