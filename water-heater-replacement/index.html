A Failed Tank Needs Fast Action.

A rusted-through tank can release forty to fifty gallons of water into a basement in a short time. Shut off the cold water valve above the heater first, then the gas or the breaker, and move anything stored on the floor.

If the leak is slow, a towel and a bucket will do while you call. If the tank is sagging or the water is hot and spreading, stay out of the area and call the 24/7 line.

Tank or Tankless for Your House.

Many homeowners replace like for like. A switch to tankless changes the gas line, venting and sometimes the budget.

Standard tank.

  • Lower upfront price.
  • Same footprint as the old unit.
  • Often the fastest replacement.
  • Holds a limited hot water supply.

Tankless.

  • Hot water on demand.
  • Takes less wall space.
  • Needs gas line and venting review.
  • Higher upfront cost.

What Comes With the New Heater.

A replacement is more than a new tank. These items are checked or renewed during every swap.

Expansion tank.

A closed plumbing system needs a place for expanded water to go. Where the home has a check valve or pressure reducing valve, an expansion tank protects the new heater and the relief valve.

Venting and combustion air.

On a gas unit the flue is checked for slope, draft and clearances. A bad vent can bring carbon monoxide into the house.

Supply lines and shutoff valves.

Old corroded connectors are replaced with new flexible lines, and a working shutoff is installed if the old one will not close.

Drain pan and relief line.

The relief discharge line is run to a safe termination, and a pan is added where a leak would reach finished space.

What Water Quality Does to the New Tank.

Chicago supplies Lake Michigan water to the South Stickney Sanitary District, and its 2013 quarterly lab reports showed hardness of 134 to 143 mg/L as calcium carbonate. The figure is dated, so check the current value with the utility.

Moderate hardness builds scale on the tank floor over the years. A yearly flush and an anode rod check are cheap ways to get more life from the new heater.

What to Do if Your Heater Leaks Before Replacement.

A leaking tank can flood a basement. These steps limit the damage.

  • Turn off the power or the gas supply to the heater.
  • Close the cold water inlet valve above the tank.
  • Run a hose from the drain valve to a floor drain if it is safe.
  • Keep children and pets away from hot water.
  • Call dispatch and say how fast the tank is leaking.

Never relight a gas heater if you smell gas.

What a Permit Check Looks For.

Replacing a water heater in Burbank can require a permit, and the City of Burbank Building Department can tell you when it does. The inspector looks at the relief valve and its discharge pipe, the gas connection and the venting, along with the expansion control.

Those items follow the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard found in 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890 and administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health and NFPA 54 for gas appliances. We build them into the quote so the permit does not arrive as a surprise.
