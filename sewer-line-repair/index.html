Sewage Backing Up: Protect the House First.

Stop using toilets, sinks and washing machines as soon as sewage shows in a floor drain or tub. Every flush adds more waste to a line that has nowhere to go. Keep children and pets away from standing water, because sewage carries bacteria.

Call the 24/7 line and describe which drains are affected. Dispatch will ask whether neighbors are backed up too, since that decides whether the problem is in your pipe or in the sanitary district main.

What Goes Wrong With Older Sewer Lines.

Failures cluster around a few causes. A camera inspection identifies which one applies.

Roots in the joints.

Roots enter through joints in clay and older concrete pipe. They block the line repeatedly and open the joint wider each time.

Cast iron corrosion.

Cast iron rusts from the inside and can flake or crack. Sections often fail where the pipe passes under a slab or near a basement wall.

Bellies and offsets.

Settling soil lets a section sag so that waste collects in the low spot. The line works until the water stands high enough to block it.

Crushed or collapsed pipe.

Heavy loads over a shallow pipe or soil movement can crush a clay line. A camera that cannot pass a point usually found one.

Heavy Rain and Basement Backups in Burbank.

The Cook County hazard mitigation plan lists federal flood disaster declarations covering Burbank in 2013 and 2023, and Illinois flood declarations in April 2013 and July 2017. It names flood-prone spots including the 8600 block of Rutherford Avenue, Narragansett Avenue between 81st and 85th Streets, and 83rd Street between Narragansett and Melvina.

When a basement backs up only during heavy rain, the line may be fine and the system overloaded. A backwater valve and a working sump pump are usually the better answer than digging up a healthy pipe.

Before the Crew Arrives.

These steps make the first visit more useful.

  • Stop all water use in the house.
  • Find the cleanout, usually a capped pipe near the basement floor drain or where the line leaves the house.
  • Write down which fixtures back up and when it began.
  • Ask neighbors whether they also have backups, and tell dispatch.
  • Keep a record of damage with photos.

If sewage has reached stored belongings, photograph everything before moving it. Insurance claims rely on documentation.

Permits and Licensed Work.

The City of Burbank Building Department says a permit is required for the construction or alteration of plumbing, and every contractor must be licensed. The office is at 6530 W 79th Street, and the number is 708-599-5500, option 2.

Standards for Repairing a Sewer Line.

Trenchless lining follows the ASTM F1216 standard for cured-in-place pipe, and open-cut replacement follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard found in 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890 and administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health. Burbank requires a permit and an inspection for sewer line work.

The South Stickney Sanitary District handles the main, and the owner is responsible for the pipe up to it. Ask dispatch where your responsibility ends before you approve a repair.
