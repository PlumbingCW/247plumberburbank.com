The First Ten Minutes of a Burst Pipe.

Speed matters more than anything else. Follow these steps in order.

1

Shut off the main.

Turn the main water valve clockwise. It is usually in the basement near the meter.

2

Cut the power.

If water is near outlets or the panel, switch off the breaker if you can reach it safely.

3

Open the taps.

Open faucets on the lowest floor to drain the lines and relieve pressure.

4

Call the line.

Describe where the water is coming from and what you have shut off.

If You Cannot Find or Turn the Main Valve.

Old gate valves can seize. Do not force them with a tool that could snap the stem. If the valve will not turn, call the 24/7 line immediately and describe the situation.

The South Stickney Sanitary District explains that a service line can also be shut off at the curb box near the street, which it calls the Buffalo Box. It takes a special key, so dispatch can advise on the next step.

Why Pipes Burst in Illinois Homes.

Burbank sees real winter cold, and older plumbing carries other risks. The causes below cover most calls.

Freeze damage.

Water expands as it freezes. Pipes in unheated garages, exterior walls and crawlspaces split where the ice forms.

Corroded galvanized steel.

Older steel supply pipe rusts from the inside until a section gives way.

High water pressure.

Pressure above normal strains fittings and fixtures until the weakest point lets go.

Failed fittings and valves.

A supply valve or a compression fitting can burst without warning, as the pipe itself stays intact.

How We Repair a Burst Pipe.

The repair depends on the material and the location.

Copper.

A damaged section is cut out and replaced with new copper and fittings, or a coupling repairs a small split. We check the pipe on either side for weakness.

Galvanized steel.

Old steel often fails in several places. Replacing a longer run or the whole line may be wiser than patching one split. See repiping for whole-house options.

PEX and plastic.

Plastic lines are repaired with approved fittings. A burst often points to freeze or a fitting failure rather than corrosion.

After the Water Stops.

Take photos of the damage before cleaning up, and keep damaged items until your insurer has seen them. Move wet furniture, run fans and dehumidifiers and open cabinet doors so that walls can dry.

If water reached drywall, insulation or flooring, call your insurance company the same day. Mold can begin within a couple of days of a wet wall.

Quick Patch or Full Repair.

A burst pipe can be stopped fast, but the permanent repair matters.

Emergency stop.

  • Shutoff and temporary clamp or cap.
  • Stops the water until parts arrive.
  • Not meant to stay in the wall.
  • Used when the right part is not on hand.

Permanent repair.

  • New pipe and fittings.
  • Pressure tested after install.
  • Leaves the wall ready for closing.
  • Done as soon as the parts are on site.

If the break came from a leak you cannot find, our leak detection service locates it before repair.

Standards for Replacement Piping.

Copper tubing is made to ASTM B88, and PEX to ASTM F876. Which one fits depends on the location, the other piping and the budget.

Repairs follow the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard found in 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890 and administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health, and the City of Burbank Building Department can tell you when a permit applies.

What to Do Until the Plumber Arrives.

The first minutes decide how much water damage you get. These steps are safe for most households.

  • Shut off the main water valve and open a faucet to relieve the pressure.
  • Turn off the power to wet areas if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  • Move furniture, rugs and valuables away from the water.
  • Take dated photos of the damage before any cleanup.
  • Do not touch outlets, cords or appliances that are wet.

Then call dispatch and describe where the water is coming from.
