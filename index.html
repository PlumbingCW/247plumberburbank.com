Who to Call First When Water Backs Up in Burbank.

Burbank homeowners often call the wrong party first, which costs time. The South Stickney Sanitary District, at 7801 Lavergne Avenue in Burbank, maintains the public sanitary sewer mains. The district says property owners are responsible beyond the main, including the connection under the parkway.

The district asks residents to call it first when a sewer backup happens so it can check the mains. If the main is clear, the problem is in the house sewer and a plumber should be called.

Not sure which side the problem is on? Call our line, and dispatch can run a cable and camera to show where the blockage sits.

What Dispatch Asks When You Call.

The first call is short and practical. Dispatch asks for the address, what is happening and when it started. They also ask whether water is spreading, whether the main valve has been shut and whether anyone is in danger.

Those answers decide whether the call is a true emergency and which crew and truck to send. If you are not sure, describe what you see and dispatch will help you decide.

What Burbank Water and Weather Do to Plumbing.

Burbank receives Lake Michigan water through the South Stickney Sanitary District, which buys from Chicago. Chicago's 2013 quarterly lab reports listed hardness between 134 and 143 mg/L as calcium carbonate, so scale builds in heaters and fixtures. That is a 2013 figure, and the district can give you the current one.

The Cook County hazard mitigation plan records federal flood declarations covering Burbank in 2013 and 2023, so storm backups and sump pump failures are a regular part of our work.

What to Do Before a Plumber Arrives.

These steps are safe for most households and limit damage while you wait.

  • Find the main water shutoff and close it if water is spreading.
  • Turn off the water heater if it is leaking.
  • Switch off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel dry.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility.
  • Photograph the damage for your records.

Then call the line and tell dispatch what you see.

Permits and Licensed Work.

According to the City of Burbank Building Department, which sets the local standard, a permit is required for construction or alteration of plumbing and that all contractors must be licensed. It is at 6530 W 79th Street, and the phone is 708-599-5500, option 2.

Illinois licenses plumbers through the Illinois Department of Public Health, and the department offers a public license verification tool. Dispatch explains how permits apply to your job.
