Leaking or Cold Water Heater: First Ten Minutes.

If water is pooling around the tank, close the cold water shutoff on the pipe above the heater. Turn the gas valve to off on a gas unit, or switch the breaker off on an electric unit, so nothing heats an empty tank.

Then call the line. Tell dispatch whether the water is coming from the top fittings, the relief valve pipe or the tank bottom, because a bottom leak almost always means the tank has failed and the next step is replacement.

Gas and Electric Tank Repairs We Handle.

Most Burbank homes run a gas or electric tank heater in the basement, utility room or garage. The repairs below cover the common failures.

Gas heaters.

Gas units depend on a clean burn and a working flue. A technician checks the pilot assembly, thermocouple, gas control valve and venting, and tests for draft before leaving the unit on.

Electric heaters.

Electric units usually fail at an upper or lower element or at a thermostat. We test the elements for continuity and replace the failed part.

Relief valves and drain valves.

The temperature and pressure relief valve is a safety part. If it drips or discharges, we check the water pressure and the expansion tank before we replace it.

Repair or Replace: Questions That Decide It.

The age and the leak location settle most decisions. Ask these before you approve a repair.

  • Is the tank itself leaking? If water is coming from the tank body, repair is not possible.
  • Is the heater near the end of a typical tank life, or has it been flushed and serviced?
  • Is the repair part a low-cost wear item such as a thermostat, element or relief valve?
  • Has the heater already needed a second repair this year?
  • Would a replacement qualify for a gas inspection and a new expansion tank at the same visit?

Our water heater replacement page covers what happens when the answer is to replace.

Hard Water Shortens Heater Life.

Chicago, which supplies water to the South Stickney Sanitary District and nearby suburbs, reported hardness between 134 and 143 mg/L as calcium carbonate in its 2013 quarterly lab reports. That is roughly 8 grains per gallon by our conversion, and it is a 2013 figure, so ask the water utility for the current number.

Moderately hard water leaves scale on heating elements and the tank floor. Regular flushing and an anode rod check slow the damage.

Standards That Apply to Water Heaters.

Temperature and pressure relief valves are made to ANSI Z21.22, and a heater that discharges through one should be inspected rather than capped. Gas models are installed and serviced against NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code that installers work from.

Ask the technician which standard applies to the part being replaced. A clear answer is a good sign.
